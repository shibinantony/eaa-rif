# AI Risk Register

Use one row per cause-event-impact scenario. Do not use broad labels such as “bias” or “security” as complete risk statements.

## Register rules

- Record impact and likelihood dimensions separately before any combined rating.
- State scale definitions and how uncertainty changes the decision.
- Link each required control to a current evidence record.
- Record treatment status independently from residual-risk acceptance.
- Expired evidence or exception changes the risk state automatically.
- Close a risk only when the exposure no longer exists or an authorized decision supersedes it; do not close because an action was assigned.
