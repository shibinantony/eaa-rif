# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they preserve the framework's independence and traceability.

## Required conventions

1. Label material as **SOURCE FRAMEWORK**, **DERIVED MAPPING**, or **ORIGINAL IP**.
2. Cite primary and authoritative sources for source-framework facts.
3. Never describe an EAA-RIF mapping as an official NIST or The Open Group crosswalk.
4. Do not reproduce copyrighted standards text, tables, or figures.
5. Record the source version and access date when a framework or tool changes.
6. Preserve separation of architecture approval, control approval, independent assurance, and residual-risk acceptance.
7. Add or update tests for machine-readable policies and schemas.

## Pull-request checklist

- [ ] Source versions are explicit.
- [ ] Proposed relationships include strength and rationale.
- [ ] New controls state their enforcement point, evidence, failure mode, owner, and exception path.
- [ ] Automation claims distinguish what is enforced from what still requires human judgment.
- [ ] No corporate or client branding, confidential data, credentials, or proprietary artifacts are present.
